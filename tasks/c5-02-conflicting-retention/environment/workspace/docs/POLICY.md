# Data retention policy

Approved by Legal, 2026-03-02. Changes need Legal sign-off.

- Free accounts: at most 90 days. Free users have not agreed to longer storage of their
  activity data, so keeping it longer is not allowed.
- Pro and Enterprise: at most 730 days, as set out in the customer agreement.

`python3 bin/validate.py` enforces these limits in CI.
